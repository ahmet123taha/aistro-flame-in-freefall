## Literal content trust and selection pipeline

1. An offline PDF.js/Python extraction builds a read-only derivative. PDF pages and XLSX sheet/row/cell text retain exact source SHA and location/offset. The runtime is pure Node and does not launch a parser, OCR or a provider tool.
2. The optional Research_Content folder belongs inside Veri_Seti. The application pins manifest SHA f3fde5c031df8f82f16c854f8b35b2f5d2a20dba0392a524126734b1864428c7 and catalog SHA 60fa421bb604c1ac9c8bf67ddb2318607434a1548eda4189e0890f0f950cad54. It authenticates the manifest, confines and authenticates shards, then rehashes selected originals through dataset.file. An absent/invalid add-on retains reviewed-source research with explicit missing coverage.
3. dataset.context retrieves against all allowed PSI/REF sources before Gemini's researchCandidates limit. Defaults are at most 24 matching shard files, ten emitted chunks, 10,000 characters including added raw context, and three emitted hits per source. The twelve-source shortlist protects up to six literal-hit sources and six sources with reviewed same-material evidence, then fills remaining slots.
4. attachSourceFragments keeps literal passages and provenance separate from the reviewed numerical index. researchContext preserves their full bounded text while augmenting coverage. researchEvidence creates stable IDs for exact source fragments; resolveResearchEvidence inserts the server's quote/location and discards model-provided provenance. Quotation membership is traceability, not semantic proof.
5. Raw table hits may include separately anchored first/preceding rows. Their role is not inferred to be a complete column header, units are not invented and rows are not forward-filled. Shared document catalog associations are context; they do not assign every finding to every linked PSI or create measurements/independent replications.
6. Literal extraction never adds experiments.rows or numerical credit automatically. Third-party Material_Sensitivity is excluded from this scientific source/evidence channel and retains applicability-only scope. The 60/40 budget remains an editorial parameter-evidence/scenario-ranking budget, not physics, fire probability or tolerance calibration.

The installed, read-back core snapshot selects 1,544 PDF/XLSX files from the 51,151-file catalog: 974 extracted, 561 PDFs with no extractable text and 9 errors. It holds 918,292 raw chunks, 12,110 PDF pages with 1,744 empty text pages, and 847,902 nonempty XLSX rows. coverageComplete=false. The extracted_unreviewed label only means text was produced, not complete or faithful transcription. Initial font/recovered-text parser warnings were not attributed per file; glyph/subscript/formula fidelity remains open and source SHA does not prove text fidelity. No scanned-image content, plotted coordinates, handwriting or generic DOCX reading is claimed. The separate reviewed DOCX adapter below does not expand general extraction format support. The unfinished broader logs build is not the tested/pinned snapshot.

## Recommendation behavior

New cards request a concrete research/design action, one short explanatory sentence, one relevance sentence and one material/condition limitation; the prompt aims for 55 words per card and usually 1–3 distinct candidates. Saved older cards may omit relevance. Source/limit stay visible; original document links use server-derived file/page/sheet/row anchors, and the source basis distinguishes an objective, hypothesis, prospective impact, study description or actual document excerpt. Prospective metadata can support reviewing a topic/hypothesis, not demonstrated effectiveness or reduced fire risk.

keep/replace/clear remain explicit and atomic. A complete independently source-validated replacement may apply when a separate source-score explanation is incomplete. Partial keep/clear stays unreviewed, and keep cannot approve a saved candidate whose original source is missing from current supplied evidence. The reducer requires the server-owned positive review flag. Environment changes invalidate older candidates and rejected/unreviewed replies retain the emergency notice. Priority orders review, not fire severity; no active-incident suppressant/dose/atmosphere intervention is validated. The modified FIF-042 clause and its previous wording are retained in requirements history.
